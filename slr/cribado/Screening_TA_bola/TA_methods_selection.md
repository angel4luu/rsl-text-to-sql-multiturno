# Selection process - title/abstract screening

Records retrieved from OpenAlex (snowballing) (n = 266), Semantic Scholar (snowballing) (n = 27) were combined and de-duplicated with the sr-screener skill (version 1.0.0) of the open-source Academic Research Skills suite (https://github.com/Imbad0202/academic-research-skills), running in Claude Code, which matches PMIDs, DOIs and normalised titles with publication year (plus or minus one year); 1 duplicates were removed, leaving 292 unique records. Titles and abstracts were screened against the pre-specified eligibility criteria of the review protocol. Each record was assessed independently by two AI reviewers (both Claude Sonnet 5.5 (claude-sonnet-5-5), prompted with different expert personas) that were given the same written protocol and decision rules and did not see each other's decisions. The reviewer agents could only read the record file they were given (no web access). Disagreements between advancing (include or unclear) and excluding a record (17 records) were resolved by a third AI reviewer (Claude Opus 5.5 (claude-opus-5-5)) that re-read the record. The two reviewers agreed on advance-versus-exclude decisions for 94.2% of records (Cohen's kappa = 0.843; prevalence-adjusted bias-adjusted kappa = 0.884).
